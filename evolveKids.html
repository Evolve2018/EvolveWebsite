What to expect

Here's exactly how drop-off and pick-up work, so you know what to expect the first time you bring your kids to Evolve.

Check-In
9:00am Service · 8:45–9:10am
10:30am Service · 10:15–10:40am
12:00pm Service · 11:45am–12:10pm

Our volunteers are ready to help with registration if it's your first time. Returning? Check your kids in and take them straight to their classroom.

During Service

Once check-in closes, all doors to children's church are locked — only approved volunteers come and go. If we ever need you, our children's coordinator will reach out directly. Our culture: learn and have fun, while staying safe.

Departure
9:00am Service · 10:00–10:10am
10:30am Service · 11:30–11:40am
12:00pm Service · 1:00–1:15pm

Please bring the wristband you received at check-in when picking up your kids. Your child's teacher will be there to help and share how much fun they had in class! Wristband required for pickup
