Good to Know

Common Questions

Anything else on your mind? Send us a note and we will help you find the right class.

Who can take a class?

Everyone. Discipleship classes are open to members and non-members alike.

How long does a class last?

Each course runs eleven weeks. You will be in the Word every week and grow alongside a small group the whole way through.

Can I take a class virtually?

Yes. You choose in person or virtual on the registration form.

Where should I start?

Faith Foundations is built for new and growing disciples. If you have walked with Jesus for a while, look at the Maturity and Equipping tracks. Still not sure? Email us and we will point you in the right direction.

I missed the deadline. What now?

Registration closes on the date listed for each class. Reach out and we will let you know when the next session opens.
