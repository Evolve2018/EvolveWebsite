SPHI / Our Approach

Housing is the beginning. Ownership is the goal.

We exist to stabilize families, restore dignity, and build a clear path to sustainable homeownership for single mothers and fathers on Milwaukee’s Northside.

How It Works

A pathway designed to end in ownership.

Participants are not rushed into buying. We build readiness step by step so ownership can last.

  2. 01

    Stable Housing

    A safe, well-kept home at an affordable rent

  3. 02

    Personal Coaching

    One-on-one guidance from entry through closing

  4. 03

    Financial Readiness

    Budgeting, credit, savings, and debt progress

  5. 04

    Grant Support

    Navigating down payment and closing grants

  6. 05

    Homeownership

    Keys in hand, and the unit opens for the next family

The objective is not indefinite dependency. It is measurable progress toward long-term stability and ownership.

What Makes Us Different

We do not hand families a packet. We walk with them.

Our program is built on personal attention, accountability, and practical training from entry through closing.

PROXIMITY
MATTERS.

Every parent has different barriers, different strengths, and a different starting point. We meet them where they are.

  • One-on-one guidance
  • Financial education
  • Homeownership preparation
  • Consistent accountability
  • Grant navigation & closing support

A Sustainable Model

Capital given once keeps creating homeowners.

Rent covers the cost of serving each family. What we need is the capital to acquire and prepare more homes.

Family after family

Step 1 of 4

Capital acquires a home

Donor gifts acquire and prepare housing, debt-free. Every home purchased with capital is a home that never carries an interest payment.

~$0

Program cost per family

Covered by the family’s own rent, so the program sustains itself.

When a family buys, the unit opens for the next family.

Homeownership Support

Down payment assistance rewards progress.

Families who successfully complete the program receive help crossing the last, hardest threshold.

Up to $0 in closing assistance

  • Funded from rent

    Assistance is drawn from rent paid during residency.

  • Earned through completion

    Released at closing when a family successfully completes the program.

  • Built for discipline

    The structure reinforces consistency, readiness, and follow-through.
