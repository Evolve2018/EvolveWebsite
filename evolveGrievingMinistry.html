Grief Is Not Something to Get Over — It's Something to Walk Through

There is no timeline, no right way, and no expiration date on grief. At Evolve, our Grieving Ministry exists to come alongside anyone who has lost someone they love — a spouse, a child, a parent, a friend — and to remind you that you were never meant to carry it by yourself.

We won't rush you. We won't hand you easy answers. We'll simply walk with you, pray with you, and make space for both your tears and your memories, for as long as you need.
