Our Foundation

What Is
Discipleship?

The ongoing mission of building disciples that follow Jesus in every aspect of their lives. A disciple is anyone under the teaching of something or someone, and as disciples of Christ, we submit our lives to His example and His Word.

Jesus emulated the nature of His Father through the Holy Spirit, and as Christ followers we share in that everlasting relationship with the Father, gifted that same Spirit to navigate life — the very Spirit that raised Christ from the dead.

Discipleship reveals the power of both, and propels us toward a life devoted to sharing this good news with others.

"And this is eternal life, that they know you, the only true God, and Jesus Christ whom you have sent." John 17:3
