What to expect

Here's exactly how drop-off and pick-up work, so you know what to expect the first time you bring your teen to Evolve.

Check-In
9:00am Service · 8:45–9:10am
10:30am Service · 10:15–10:40am
12:00pm Service · 11:45am–12:10pm

Our team will greet you and your teen at check-in and get them settled in. First time here? We'll walk you through everything.

During Service

Once check-in closes, our space is locked down — only approved leaders come and go. If we ever need you, our team will reach out directly. Safe, fun, and focused on your teen the whole time.

Departure
9:00am Service · 10:00–10:10am
10:30am Service · 11:30–11:40am
12:00pm Service · 1:00–1:15pm

Please bring the sticker you received at check-in when picking up your teen. Sticker required for pickup
