When you give

You Show Up for People When It Matters Most

Giving at Evolve is more than a transaction. It is worship, and it is how we Love God, Love People and Love Ourselves together.

Your generosity keeps the doors open every Sunday, puts mentors in front of kids and teens, walks with families through grief and hard seasons, and helps single parents find stable housing.

When you invest in Evolve, you are investing in the people of Milwaukee and in a church that has always wanted to be known for giving back.
