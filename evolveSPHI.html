The Program

A real path to ownership

We provide a furnished home, a dedicated team, and up to 12 months to complete the journey to owning a home of your own.

01
🏠

Move into a program home

Rent a beautiful, partially furnished Evolve home near our North Milwaukee campus while you build toward homeownership.

02
📈

Build your financial foundation

Work with our team and partners like Self-Help Federal Credit Union to strengthen your credit, savings, and mortgage readiness.

03
🔑

Find and purchase your home

With up to 12 months of support and coaching, identify and close on a home that belongs to your family.

04
🎉

Receive your grants

Receive a $5,000 homeownership grant and a $2,000 furniture allowance upon successfully completing the program.

Eligibility

Do you qualify?

We look for single parents ready to commit to the process. Here is what is required to apply.

Personal Requirements

  • Single parent with at least one biological or adopted child
  • Adult children are eligible; all must be on prior year tax return
  • Decent rental history (eviction must be at least 24 months prior)
  • Provide 3 rental references
  • Sign rental agreement and program contract including media release

Financial Requirements

  • 3 years of stable employment history
  • Gross income at least 3x monthly rent ($3,300 minimum)
  • Debt to income ratio within program limit
  • Minimum 580 credit score
  • Bankruptcy accepted (2 to 4 years prior, must be loan-eligible)
  • Current on taxes, no liens or pending judgements
  • Pass criminal background and credit check

Not sure if you qualify? Call (414) 510-4131 or email sphi@evolvenation.org — we'll walk through your situation personally. We are a church before we are a program.
